Methodological guardrails

Registered voters, voting age population, eligible voters, early votes and certified ballots cast are distinct measures. Party and race registration do not establish candidate preference. Parish early voting cannot be assigned to congressional districts without a documented geographic crosswalk. No forecast or winner probabilities are presented.
