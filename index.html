What a court ruling means for representation

A challenge, an injunction, a stay, a final decision, and a replacement law are different events. A boundary change does not automatically remove sitting representatives or invalidate prior election results. Any election consequences depend on the specific operative order and applicable law.

No 2026 Act 2 invalidation is inferred from the ruling on 2024 Act 2. The tracker does not claim comprehensive coverage of litigation or continuously updated court status.

Methodological guardrails

Registered voters, voting age population, eligible voters, early votes and certified ballots cast are distinct measures. Party and race registration do not establish candidate preference. Parish early voting cannot be assigned to congressional districts without a documented geographic crosswalk. No forecast or winner probabilities are presented.
